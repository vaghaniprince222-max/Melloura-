Our Heritage
is our Craft

Born from a reverence for the grand confiseries of Brussels and Paris, Melloura was founded to resurrect the sacred art of chocolate making. We reject mass production in favor of meticulous small-batch creation, honoring centuries-old techniques perfected by European masters.

Every ganache is infused slowly, every praline layered with patience, and every truffle shaped by skilled human hands. We source single-origin cocoa from ethical, biodiversity-focused farms across Ecuador, Madagascar, and Venezuela—ensuring that every bite honors both tradition and the earth.
